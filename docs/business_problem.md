# Business Problem & Lifecycle

## 1. Future Customer Lifecycle
The platform will model the customer journey through the following distinct stages. Understanding this lifecycle is critical, as data is produced and updated at each transition.

1. **Lead**: A prospective borrower who has been targeted by marketing or has visited the platform. 
   * *Data Produced*: Marketing campaign interactions, web tracking data, initial credit soft-pull.
2. **Application**: The lead submits a formal request for a loan.
   * *Data Produced*: Requested loan amount, stated income, employment details, loan purpose, credit bureau hard-pull data (FICO, DTI, open accounts).
3. **Approval**: The credit risk model evaluates the application and issues a decision (Approve/Reject).
   * *Data Produced*: Risk grade, sub-grade, approved interest rate, assigned installment amount, predicted probability of default.
4. **Funding**: The loan is issued to the customer and is active.
   * *Data Produced*: Issue date, funded amount, initial list status.
5. **Repayment**: The customer makes regular monthly installments.
   * *Data Produced*: Payment dates, principal received, interest received, late fees, current outstanding principal.
6. **Collections**: A customer misses payments and enters delinquency or default.
   * *Data Produced*: Delinquency status, days past due, collections fees, recovery amounts.
7. **Closed**: The loan lifecycle ends, either via full payoff or charge-off.
   * *Data Produced*: Final loan status (Fully Paid / Charged Off), total return.

## 2. Expected Business Entities
The analytical platform will rely on a normalized view of the following core business entities:

### Customer
* **Purpose**: Represents the individual borrower. Central to customer lifetime value (CLV) and behavioral modeling.
* **Important Attributes**: `member_id`, `annual_inc`, `emp_length`, `home_ownership`, `addr_state`, `fico_range_low`.
* **Future Relationships**: 1:N with Loans, 1:N with Marketing Campaigns.

### Loan
* **Purpose**: Represents the credit product issued to the customer. Core entity for credit risk modeling and portfolio management.
* **Important Attributes**: `loan_amnt`, `term`, `int_rate`, `grade`, `issue_d`, `loan_status`.
* **Future Relationships**: N:1 with Customer, 1:N with Payments, 1:1 with Prediction.

### Payment
* **Purpose**: Represents an individual financial transaction made towards a loan.
* **Important Attributes**: `last_pymnt_d`, `last_pymnt_amnt`, `total_rec_prncp`, `total_rec_int`.
* **Future Relationships**: N:1 with Loan.

### Marketing Campaign
* **Purpose**: Represents targeted efforts to acquire leads. (Synthetic data will be generated for this).
* **Important Attributes**: `campaign_id`, `channel`, `spend`, `target_segment`.
* **Future Relationships**: 1:N with Customers (Leads).

### Prediction
* **Purpose**: Stores the output of machine learning models for historical tracking and model monitoring.
* **Important Attributes**: `prediction_id`, `model_version`, `predicted_default_prob`, `timestamp`.
* **Future Relationships**: 1:1 with Application/Loan.

## 3. Business KPIs
The platform will calculate the following Key Performance Indicators to monitor business health.

### Credit Risk KPIs
* **Approval Rate**: 
  * *Calculation*: Total Approved Applications / Total Applications.
  * *Value*: Monitors credit tightening/loosening.
* **Default Rate**: 
  * *Calculation*: Total Defaulted Loans / Total Active Loans.
  * *Value*: Core metric of credit model accuracy.
* **Charge-Off Rate**: 
  * *Calculation*: Total Value of Charged-Off Loans / Total Portfolio Value.
  * *Value*: Direct measure of financial loss.
* **Delinquency Rate**: 
  * *Calculation*: Loans 30+ Days Past Due / Total Active Loans.
  * *Value*: Leading indicator of future defaults.
* **Recovery Rate**: 
  * *Calculation*: Total Recovered Amount / Total Charged-Off Amount.
  * *Value*: Measures the effectiveness of the collections process.
* **Portfolio Exposure**: 
  * *Calculation*: Sum of Outstanding Principal across all active loans.
  * *Value*: Measures total capital at risk.
* **Average Loan Size**: 
  * *Calculation*: Total Funded Amount / Number of Loans.
  * *Value*: Indicates portfolio composition and risk concentration.

### Customer & Marketing KPIs
* **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV/LTV)**: 
  * *Calculation*: Net Present Value of all profit from a customer (Interest - Defaults - Acquisition Cost).
  * *Value*: Guides maximum allowable customer acquisition cost.
* **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: 
  * *Calculation*: Total Marketing Spend / Total Funded Loans.
  * *Value*: Efficiency of the marketing funnel.
* **Campaign ROI**: 
  * *Calculation*: (CLV generated by Campaign - Campaign Spend) / Campaign Spend.
  * *Value*: Determines marketing budget allocation.
* **Repeat Borrower Rate**: 
  * *Calculation*: Customers with >1 Loan / Total Customers.
  * *Value*: Measures customer satisfaction and stickiness.
* **Customer Retention**: 
  * *Calculation*: % of eligible users who take a second loan within X months.
  * *Value*: Core growth metric.

## 4. Business & Strategy Assumptions
* **Business Assumptions**: We assume the platform operates as an originator holding loans on the balance sheet. The primary revenue driver is interest margin minus expected credit losses.
* **Synthetic Marketing Data**: The base LendingClub dataset lacks marketing touchpoints. To demonstrate full Customer Analytics, we will inject synthetic data (Campaigns, CAC, Web tracking) upstream to join with the empirical loan data.
